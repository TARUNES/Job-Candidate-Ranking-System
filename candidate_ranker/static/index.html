Step 1
Load Machine Learning Models
Initialize ML inference engines from local disk cache.

Step 2
Parse Job Description (JD)
Extract required/preferred skills, target experience, and location constraints from the job description.
Flan-T5 model executes dynamic entity extraction templates on the unstructured JD text, parsing specific criteria into strict structured properties.
Pending
Step 3
Generate Job Description Semantic Embedding
Encode the parsed job description into a high-dimensional vector.
SentenceTransformer maps the processed JD criteria text to a normalized dense vector to enable semantic similarity checks.
Pending
Step 4
Stream Candidates & Apply Hard Heuristic Filters
Iterate candidate records and filter out profiles with hard disqualifying signals.
JSONLines records are streamed sequentially. Implements strict notice period constraints, schema-validation rules, and impossible-skills honeypot filters to avoid scoring overhead.
Pending
Step 5
Run TF-IDF Domain Mismatch Detector
Identify profiles where the candidate's headline and career history diverge.
Fits a data-driven TF-IDF vectorizer over the pooled candidate text profiles, analyzing vocabulary coherence to catch fraud/mismatched domains.
Pending
Step 6
Screen & Pre-rank (Non-Semantic Score)
Evaluate candidates by experience curves, location hub scores, notice periods, and redrob trust signals.
Computes non-semantic composite scores dynamically scaled to the parsed JD requirements. Shortlists the top 2000 profiles for the deep semantic stage.
Pending
Step 7
Encode Semantic Vectors for Shortlisted Candidates
Extract candidate text profiles and encode their dense vectors.
Extracts candidate headline, skills, and roles, truncating to 600 characters for high-performance batched SentenceTransformer encoding.
Pending
Step 8
Compute Hybrid Scoring & Select Top 100
Calculate cosine similarities and combine semantic and heuristic scoring dimensions.
Executes vectorized matrix cosine similarity computation. Hybrid formula: 0.45 * semantic + 0.55 * non-semantic composite.
Pending
Step 9
Generate Grounded Reasoning Justifications
Generate concise reasons justifying the final rank of the top 100 profiles.
Examines matching required and preferred skills, comparing them with years of experience to output customized, grounded reasoning sentences.
